Restaurants

Lincoln punches above its weight for food. The Haymarket and downtown O Street corridor have the density — but some of the best spots are scattered across the city. Local-first, no chains.

By Zach
Local-first
No chain defaults

Breakfast & Brunch

Morning Fuel

Lincoln's brunch game is serious. The Haymarket district has the highest concentration, but a few essential spots are dotted around the city. Get here early on weekends — waits are real.

The Green Gateau
🥐⭐ 4.7
Breakfast · 330 S 10th St · Lincoln Institution

The Green Gateau has been a Lincoln breakfast institution for decades — the kind of place that regulars have gone to every Sunday for 20 years and intend to keep doing so forever 🥐. House-baked pastries, fresh quiches, creamy soups, and a brunch menu that's been quietly dialed-in to near-perfection. The building itself is a converted 1890s home. Charming in a way that doesn't try too hard. Arrive 15 minutes before opening on weekends if you want a table.

🏆 Order: Quiche of the day · House pastries · The soup
📍 Nearby: Nebraska State Capitol is a 10-min walk — combine for a morning → Culture
$$ · 330 S 10th St · Mon–Fri 11am–2pm & 5–9pm · Sat 8am–9pm · Sun 8am–3pm ↗ Maps
Runza
🥟⭐ 4.4
Nebraska Original · Multiple Locations · The State Food

If you don't eat a Runza while in Nebraska, you haven't been to Nebraska. This is the state's homegrown fast-food chain — a warm, doughy bread pocket stuffed with seasoned beef, cabbage, and onions 🥟. Born in Lincoln in 1949. The chain sandwich is fine but the Runza itself is the thing. It's comfort food that makes zero sense on paper and complete sense in your hands at 11am. Order the original. Get onion rings.

🏆 Order: Original Runza · Onion rings · It's $7 — just do it
$ · Multiple Lincoln locations · Drive-through available · Nebraska staple ↗ Maps

Lunch & Casual

Midday Moves

Lincoln's lunch scene is dominated by the Haymarket and O Street. These are the spots worth a real stop — not just fuel.

Honest Abe's Burgers & Freedom
🍔⭐ 4.6
Smash Burgers · O Street · Lincoln Best Burger

The best burger in Lincoln, and it's not close. Thin smash patties, maximally caramelized crust, stacked with serious toppings on a brioche bun 🍔. The kitchen is tiny, the menu is focused, and the execution is consistent. There's a reason the line moves out the door on weekends. Their fries are also legitimately excellent — not an afterthought. Get there by 11:30 if you want to eat before the lunch crowd descends.

🏆 Order: Double smash burger · Fries · A shake if you're committed
📍 Nearby: Zoo Bar is a short walk for a post-lunch beer → Bars
$ · Multiple Lincoln locations · Mon–Sun 11am–9pm ↗ Maps
Bison Witches Bar & Deli
🥪⭐ 4.5
Deli · Haymarket · Giant Sandwiches · Cold Beer

Half deli, half bar — and both halves are excellent. Massive sandwiches on house-baked bread, a rotating tap list that leans Nebraska craft, and a cozy Haymarket space that fills up fast at lunch 🥙. The Reuben is the move, full stop. Cold beer and a big sandwich is a simple formula but Bison Witches executes it better than anyone else in the city. Crowded on game days — plan accordingly.

🏆 Order: The Reuben · Whatever Nebraska craft is on tap · Get there before noon
📍 Nearby: D.C. Haymakers is around the corner for a post-lunch drink → Bars
$$ · 1320 P St · Mon–Wed 10:30am–9pm · Thu–Sat 10:30am–10pm · Sun 10:30am–9pm ↗ Maps
Lazlo's Brewery & Grill
🍺⭐ 4.4
Brewpub · Haymarket · All-Day · Reliable

Lincoln's original brewpub and still one of the most reliable all-day spots in the city. The house-brewed beers are genuinely good — especially the IPA and lager. The menu is broader than most brewpubs: burgers, ribs, pasta, and a prime rib that's earned its reputation 🥩. Go for the beer-and-food combo. Central Haymarket location makes it an easy anchor for any afternoon.

🏆 Order: House IPA · Smoked prime rib sandwich · Onion rings
$$ · 210 N 7th St · Mon–Thu 11am–10:30pm · Fri–Sat 11am–11pm · Sun 11am–10:30pm ↗ Maps

International & Specialty

Beyond Burgers

Lincoln is more diverse than its reputation suggests. There's legitimately excellent Indian, Thai, Vietnamese, and Ethiopian food here — seek it out.

The Oven
🍛⭐ 4.6
Indian · 201 N 8th St · Haymarket · Best Indian in Nebraska

The best Indian restaurant in Nebraska, and legitimately one of the best in the Midwest — not a small claim. Run with serious commitment to authentic regional Indian cuisine: the tandoori preparations are exceptional, the curries have real depth, and the lunch buffet is one of the great weekday deals in the city 🍛. The naan from the tandoor oven is exactly what it should be. Dinner is busier; the lunch buffet gives you more variety for less money. Don't miss the mango lassi.

🏆 Order: Chicken tikka masala · Lamb rogan josh · Garlic naan · Mango lassi
📍 Nearby: Haymarket district is steps away — explore before or after → Culture
$$ · 201 N 8th St · Mon dinner only 5–9pm · Tue–Sat 11:30am–2pm & 5–10pm · Sun 5–9pm ↗ Maps
Vincenzo's Ristorante
🍝⭐ 4.5
Italian · 808 P St · Haymarket · Classic · Dinner

Lincoln's classic Italian restaurant — in business long enough to have become part of the city's identity. Handmade pastas, veal dishes, excellent tiramisu, and a wine list that takes itself appropriately seriously 🍷. The dining room has that warm, slightly formal old-school Italian vibe. Not trendy, intentionally. The kind of restaurant your parents consider "going out" and they're right to. Reservations on weekends.

🏆 Order: House-made pasta · Veal piccata · Tiramisu · Glass of Barolo
$$$ · 808 P St · Mon–Fri 11am–2pm & 4–10pm · Sat 4–10pm · Sun 4–9pm · Reserve weekends ↗ Maps
Pho Factory
🍜⭐ 4.6
Vietnamese · 940 N 26th St · Pho · Banh Mi

Lincoln's go-to for Vietnamese pho. The broth is slow-cooked and genuinely restorative, the banh mi is solid, and the portions are serious 🍜. A reliable neighborhood spot that locals keep coming back to. Order the large pho and commit. Vietnamese iced coffee is mandatory.

🏆 Order: Large beef pho · Banh mi · Vietnamese iced coffee
$ · 940 N 26th St · Mon–Sat 11am–9pm · Sun 10am–8pm ↗ Maps

Dinner & Splurge

Worth It

For when you want to actually sit down, take your time, and eat something that makes you feel things. Lincoln has options beyond Applebee's, promise.

Venue Restaurant & Lounge
🔥⭐ 4.7
Upscale American · South Lincoln · Special Occasion

Lincoln's go-to for a proper dinner out. The filet is consistently great, the cocktail program is serious, and the warm interior makes the whole thing feel like somewhere worth being 🥩. Service is attentive without being overbearing. Good for date night, celebrations, or any dinner where you want to feel like an adult. The beef tenderloin and the pork belly starter are the moves. Reserve ahead.

🏆 Order: Filet mignon · Pork belly starter · House cocktail
📍 Nearby: Leadbelly or Single Barrel for a pre-dinner cocktail → Bars
$$$ · 4111 Pioneer Woods Dr · Mon–Sat 11am–10pm · Sun closed · Reserve recommended ↗ Maps
Plank Seafood Provisions
🦞⭐ 4.8
Seafood · Haymarket · Daily Catch · Chef-Driven

Fresh seafood in landlocked Nebraska sounds like a trap — Plank is the exception that disproves the rule. Daily fish flown in, serious kitchen execution, and a menu that changes based on what's actually good that week 🐟. The oyster selection is impressive for a city this far from any coast. The whole fish preparation is always the best thing on the menu. Come hungry and order multiple things. This is worth the spend.

🏆 Order: Oysters on the half shell · Whole fish of the day · The chowder
$$$ · Haymarket · Dinner · Daily catch menu · Reserve ahead ↗ Maps
Misty's Restaurant & Lounge
🥩⭐ 4.5
Prime Rib · 70th & Van Dorn · Lincoln Institution · Since 1966

Been slinging prime rib since 1966 and shows absolutely zero signs of slowing down. This is the place Lincoln goes for a celebratory dinner — Husker wins, anniversaries, graduations 🎉. The prime rib is carved tableside and sized in a way that requires a moment of reckoning. The salad bar is a full experience in itself (in the best possible '70s supper-club way). Order the queen cut if you're not sure. Order the king cut if you're sure.

🏆 Order: Prime rib · Hit the salad bar · Old fashioned from the bar
$$$ · 6235 Havelock Ave · Tue–Thu 4–9pm · Fri–Sat 4–10pm · Sun 4–9pm · Mon closed ↗ Maps
Leadbelly
🍹⭐ 4.8
Cocktail Bar + Kitchen · Lively · Downtown · Creative Menu

One of Lincoln's best cocktail bars that's also producing serious food — that combination is rarer than it should be. The bar team is legitimately talented: seasonal, ingredient-driven cocktails that justify the price 🍸. The food menu rotates but has ranged from excellent charcuterie to some of the most creative small plates in the city. Get a few rounds of both. The vibe is lively without being a scene. Weekends can get crowded.

🏆 Order: House seasonal cocktail · Charcuterie board · Small plates to share
$$$ · 301 N 8th St · Mon–Thu 11am–9pm · Fri–Sun 11am–10pm ↗ Maps
Single Barrel
🥃⭐ 4.6
Whiskey Bar + Kitchen · Haymarket · Craft Cocktails

Whiskey-focused bar and kitchen in the Haymarket that takes the spirits side of things seriously without being insufferable about it 🥃. The food menu was designed to pair with whiskey — smoked meats, elevated bar snacks, and a burger worth writing home about. Extensive bourbon and Scotch selection. Great for a pre-dinner drink that turns into dinner.

🏆 Order: A neat pour of something interesting · Smoked brisket · The burger
$$$ · 141 N 9th St (Graduate Hotel) · Mon–Thu 7am–10pm · Fri 7am–midnight · Sat 7am–midnight · Sun 7am–10pm ↗ Maps
Boitano's
🍕⭐ 4.5
Pizza · Late Night · Downtown · Thin Crust

Lincoln's best late-night pizza. The thin crust is properly done — crispy edge, good char, not a cracker but not a chew toy either 🍕. Classic combinations done right rather than trying too hard to be innovative. It's the kind of pizza you want at 10pm when the question is "pizza or going home?" The calzones are underrated. Walk-in friendly most of the time.

🏆 Order: Thin crust pepperoni · Calzone · Works after a night at the bars
$$ · Downtown · Late night · Pizza + calzones ↗ Maps
🌽 Lincoln tip: The Haymarket has the highest restaurant density — but some of the city's best spots (Misty's, Vincenzo's, Pho Factory) are scattered around. Worth a short drive for the right meal.

Bars

Lincoln's bar scene is anchored by the Haymarket and the O Street corridor. From a 100-year-old dive to a world-class blues venue to serious craft cocktail programs — it's all here.

No affiliation

Iconic & Historic

Lincoln Legends

Some bars are just places to drink. These are places that are part of the city's actual identity.

The Zoo Bar
🎸⭐ 4.8
Blues Bar · 136 N 14th St · Since 1973 · National Landmark

One of America's great blues bars, full stop. In continuous operation since 1973, the Zoo Bar has hosted some of the most important names in American blues — many before anyone else knew them 🎷. It's a narrow, dark, loud, beautiful room with a stage small enough that you're 10 feet from whatever's happening. Live music almost every night. Cold Pabst. Sticky floor. No irony. If you only do one bar in Lincoln, it's this one. Check the schedule ahead of time — the weekly lineups are posted online.

🏆 Do: Show up for a live show · Order Pabst · Stand close to the stage
📍 Nearby: Honest Abe's Burgers is a short walk for pre-show fuel → Restaurants
$ · 136 N 14th St · Mon–Thu 3pm–1am · Fri–Sat 3pm–2am · Sun noon–2am · 21+ after 9pm ↗ Maps
Duffy's Tavern
🍻⭐ 4.5
Dive Bar · 1412 O St · Lincoln's Oldest · No Frills

Lincoln's oldest and most beloved dive bar. The kind of place where nobody cares what you're wearing, the beer is cheap, the jukebox is actually good, and the regulars have been sitting on the same stools for 30 years 🍺. Dark, comfortable, and completely unpretentious. Duffy's has outlasted every trend in Lincoln's bar scene because it never tried to follow one. Pool table, strong pours. Go on a Tuesday when it's actually a neighborhood bar and not just a landmark.

🏆 Do: Cheap beer · Jukebox · Pool · Sit at the bar and talk to someone
📍 Nearby: Zoo Bar is right next door — do both in one night
$ · 1412 O St · Daily 4pm–2am · Live music Wed & Sun · Cash preferred ↗ Maps
Knickerbockers
🎵⭐ 4.6
Live Music Venue · Punk · Indie · Alternative · All Ages

Lincoln's indie/punk/alternative live music mainstay. Small, slightly chaotic, and exactly as it should be — the shows here have energy that bigger venues can't manufacture 🎤. A mix of local bands and touring acts that are one album away from playing much larger rooms. All-ages for shows, 21+ at the bar. Check the calendar; it books up.

$ · 901 O St · Live music venue · Check schedule · All-ages shows ↗ Maps

Haymarket Strip

Historic District
D.C. Haymakers
🌾⭐ 4.6
Bar + Kitchen · Haymarket · Outdoor Patio · All Day

Haymarket anchor bar with a great patio and a food menu that goes beyond standard bar fare. Cold craft beer, solid cocktails, and a vibe that works for a midday patio session or a late-evening hang 🌳. One of the few Haymarket spots where the outdoor seating is genuinely pleasant rather than just an afterthought. Get here in the afternoon on a nice day and you won't leave until evening.

📍 Nearby: The Oven and Lazlo's are steps away → Restaurants
$$ · Haymarket · All day · Patio · Bar + kitchen ↗ Maps
HiPo
🍸⭐ 4.8
Craft Cocktails · Intimate · Haymarket · Bar-Forward

Lincoln's most serious craft cocktail bar. Small, intimate, dimly lit — and the people behind the bar know what they're doing 🍸. The cocktail menu rotates seasonally and shows genuine creativity without being precious about it. This is where you go when you want to drink something properly made rather than something with a blinking light in it. Sit at the bar. Ask what they're excited about. Let them make something for you.

🏆 Do: Sit at the bar · Ask for a recommendation · Order something you've never had
$$ · Haymarket area · Verify hours before visiting · Cocktail-focused · Intimate ↗ Maps
Barry's Bar & Grill
🏆⭐ 4.5
Sports Bar · O Street · Multiple TVs · Classic

The O Street sports bar that actually delivers. Multiple screens, every game, decent food (the burger is better than it needs to be), and a crowd that's actually into whatever's on rather than treating sports as background noise 📺. Game day at Barry's has a proper energy. The loaded fries are the move. Get there early for any big Husker game — it fills fast and then some.

🏆 Order: Loaded fries · Cold beer · Sit facing the biggest screen
$$ · O Street · Sports bar · All games · Game day institution ↗ Maps

After Hours
The Railyard
🏙️⭐ 4.4
Entertainment District · Multiple Venues · Outdoor · O Street Corridor

Lincoln's outdoor entertainment hub — a converted rail yard (appropriately) with multiple bars, restaurants, and a live music stage. Nature & Parks

Lincoln is a surprisingly green city — it has more park acreage per capita than most comparably-sized American cities. The surrounding prairie and lakes make for easy day trips that feel like you're far from anywhere.

Urban Parks

In the City

Lincoln's park system is legitimately impressive. Wilderness Park alone — 1,400+ acres of trails inside city limits — is one of the great urban natural areas in America.

Wilderness Park
🌲⭐ 4.7
Trail System · 1,400+ Acres · Largest Urban Park in US · Free

The largest municipal urban park in the United States — 1,400+ contiguous acres of mixed hardwood forest, prairie, and creek bottom entirely within Lincoln's city limits 🌲. Multiple entry points, 20+ miles of multi-use trails, mountain bike loops, and enough tree cover that you can be 30 minutes in and feel completely removed from the city. The Salt Creek corridor trail system runs through it. No admission, open daily. Bring water — the trails are further than they look. Best in spring/fall when the canopy is dramatic.

🏆 Do: South entry trail toward the creek · Sunrise or golden hour run · Mountain bike the north loops
Free · Multiple entries · 20+ miles of trails · All skill levels ↗ Maps
Pioneers Park & Nature Center
🦬⭐ 4.7
Bison · Elk · Prairie · Nature Center · West Lincoln

A 668-acre park on the western edge of Lincoln with a nature center, native prairie restoration, and the most surprising feature: actual American bison and elk roaming behind natural fencing 🦬. This is not a zoo. It's a preservation landscape. The bison pasture is visible from the walking trails. The nature center has well-curated exhibits on Great Plains ecology and Indigenous history. Peaceful, underused, genuinely beautiful on a clear morning with low-angle prairie light.

🏆 Do: Walk the bison prairie trail · Nature center exhibits · Sunrise on the prairie
📍 Nearby: Sunken Gardens is a short drive → Nature + Culture combo day
Free entry · Nature center has hours · West Lincoln · All ages ↗ Maps
Sunken Gardens
🌸⭐ 4.8
Public Garden · 27th & Capitol Pkwy · Free · Peak: June–Sept

One of the most beautiful public gardens in the Midwest — and almost no one outside of Lincoln knows it exists. The sunken design creates a natural amphitheater of color: 30,000+ annuals and perennials planted in rotating seasonal displays, koi pond, fountains 🌺. Best from late June through September. Free, open daily, walkable from downtown. Legitimately worth going out of your way for if you have any appreciation for gardens. Photography gold at any hour.

🏆 Do: Evening walk when the lighting is warm · Summer peak color · Bring a camera
Free · 27th & Capitol Pkwy · Open daily · Peak season June–Sept ↗ Maps
Holmes Lake Park
🎣⭐ 4.6
Lake · Southeast Lincoln · Fishing · Walking Path · Disc Golf

Lincoln's most popular urban lake park — a 90-acre lake surrounded by a well-maintained trail loop, fishing access, disc golf course, and open meadow space 🎣. Largemouth bass, bluegill, and catfish fishing. Kayak and canoe launch available. The perimeter trail (about 2.5 miles) is one of the city's best walks — the lake view is genuinely pleasant in any season. Disc golf course is one of the better free urban courses in the state.

🏆 Do: Perimeter trail walk · Throw a disc golf round · Fish at dawn
Free · Southeast Lincoln · Fishing license required · Kayak launch ↗ Maps

Day Trips

Under 1 Hour

Some of Nebraska's best outdoor experiences are within an hour of Lincoln. Worth the drive.

Branched Oak Lake
⛵⭐ 4.8
Lake · ~30 min NW · Swimming · Sailing · Camping · Beach

The best lake day from Lincoln. 1,800+ acres of Nebraska reservoir with a sandy beach, designated swimming area, boat launch, sailing club, camping, and fishing all in one spot ⛵. Crystal-clear water compared to most Nebraska lakes. The beach gets proper summer energy. Sailboats are out most weekends from April to October. Camping is excellent — reserve early for summer weekends. This is the lake Lincoln goes to when it wants to feel like it's somewhere with water.

🏆 Do: Beach day with picnic · Rent a kayak or bring a boat · Sunset from the beach
Park pass required · ~30 min NW of Lincoln · Beach + boat + camping ↗ Maps
Indian Cave State Park
🏞️⭐ 4.8
State Park · ~1.5 hrs SE · Bluffs · Missouri River · Hiking

The most scenic hiking in eastern Nebraska, by a wide margin. Limestone bluffs and ravines carved by the Missouri River, 20+ miles of wooded trails, a replica 1800s pioneer town, and the actual sandstone cave with Native American petroglyphs that gives the park its name 🏞️. The overlook views of the Missouri River bottom are dramatic — you forget you're in Nebraska for a minute. Worth the 1.5-hour drive. Go on a weekday if possible — it's popular and the parking lots fill on summer weekends.

🏆 Do: Bluff Trail overlooks · The cave itself · Camp if you can stay overnight
Park pass · ~1.5 hrs SE · Camping available · Best hiking in eastern NE ↗ Maps
Spring Creek Prairie Audubon Center
🦅⭐ 4.7
Native Prairie · ~25 min SW · Birdwatching · Trails · Quiet

A rare intact piece of native tallgrass prairie — the ecosystem that once covered most of the Great Plains and now exists on less than 1% of its original range. The Audubon Center manages 800+ acres of restored and preserved prairie 🦅. Walking trails wind through grasses that grow 6 feet tall in summer. Exceptional birdwatching (short-eared owls, bobolinks, dickcissels). The kind of place that recalibrates what you think Nebraska actually looks like. Quiet, meditative, beautiful.

🏆 Do: Dawn bird walk · Prairie bloom season (July) · Bring binoculars
Small entry fee · ~25 min SW of Lincoln · Native tallgrass prairie · Birdwatching ↗ Maps

Architecture & Museums

Worth Your Time

Lincoln's cultural institutions are routinely underestimated. The State Capitol alone justifies the visit.

Nebraska State Capitol
🏛️⭐ 4.9
Architecture · 1932 Art Deco · Free Tours · Tallest in the US

One of the great buildings in America — full stop. The Nebraska State Capitol (completed 1932) is an extraordinary Art Deco tower on the Great Plains, designed by Bertram Goodhue to be something genuinely original rather than a Washington D.C. copy 🏛️. 400-foot tower, Sower statue on top, mosaics by Hildreth Meière, and interior work that is legitimately world-class. Free tours run regularly and are worth taking — the rotunda and legislative chambers alone are worth the trip. Look up constantly inside. Don't rush this.

🏆 Do: Take the guided tour · Look up in the rotunda · The observation deck on a clear day
📍 Nearby: The Green Gateau is a 10-min walk for breakfast before the tour → Restaurants
Free · 1445 K St · Daily tours · Observation deck · Art Deco masterpiece ↗ Maps
Sheldon Museum of Art
🎨⭐ 4.8
Art Museum · UNL Campus · Free · Philip Johnson Building

One of the most significant university art museums in America — the Philip Johnson building alone (1963) is a work of architecture worth the visit, and the collection inside is serious 🎨. Permanent holdings include major American artists and rotating exhibitions that would be strong in any major city. Free admission. Right on the UNL campus with excellent sculpture garden. Open Tuesday–Sunday. The kind of museum that gets better the slower you move through it. Don't skip the sculpture garden.

🏆 Do: Walk the sculpture garden · Rotating exhibition · Architecture as much as art inside
Free · UNL Campus · Tue–Wed/Fri–Sat 10am–5pm · Thu 10am–7pm · Sun noon–5pm · Mon closed ↗ Maps
International Quilt Museum
🪡⭐ 4.8
World's Largest Quilt Collection · UNL Campus · Unexpected Masterpiece

The world's largest public quilt collection — and yes, it's serious. If you walked in expecting folk art and left understanding you just saw one of the great textile collections in the world, that's the normal reaction 🪡. 5,000+ quilts representing centuries of the art form across cultures, with dedicated permanent galleries and rotating exhibitions. The building itself is architecturally striking. Consistently surprises people who weren't expecting to care.

🏆 Do: Historic quilts gallery · Whatever's on rotating exhibit · Budget 2 hours
Small entry fee · UNL campus · Tue–Sat 10am–4pm · Mon/Sun closed ↗ Maps
Nebraska History Museum
📜⭐ 4.6
State History · Downtown · Free · Native American Collections

The official repository of Nebraska's story — and it's more interesting than it sounds. Strong Native American collections (Great Plains nations, particularly), pioneer and settlement history, and the kind of deep regional archival material that makes actual historians excited 📜. Free admission. The building is a historic landmark itself. Worth 90 minutes if you have any interest in what this place was before it was a state. Don't skip the Indigenous history galleries.

🏆 Do: Indigenous history galleries · Pioneer collection · Free and genuinely informative
Free · 131 Centennial Mall N · Tue–Fri 10am–4pm · Sat 10am–2pm · Mon/Sun closed ↗ Maps

Districts & Events

Weekly + Seasonal
Lincoln Haymarket Farmer's Market
🥬⭐ 4.8
Market · Haymarket · Every Saturday · May–October · 8am–noon

One of the best farmer's markets in the Midwest — not hyperbole. Every Saturday from May through October, the Haymarket blocks fill with 250+ vendors: Nebraska farm produce, artisan food products, live music, local food trucks, and an energy that makes it hard to leave before noon 🥦. The best time to be in the Haymarket. Go hungry, bring a bag, talk to the vendors. This is what the city does well on a Saturday morning.

🏆 Do: Arrive early for the best produce · Grab breakfast from a food vendor · Get honey from a local beekeeper
📍 Nearby: Lazlo's Brewery & Grill is steps away for brunch or lunch after → Restaurants

Haymarket Historic District
🧱⭐ 4.7
Historic District · Brick Warehouses · Shops + Restaurants + Bars

The engine of Lincoln's cultural and culinary scene — a 10-block historic district of late 1800s brick warehouses converted into restaurants, bars, galleries, boutiques, and public space 🧱. The scale is walkable and human. The architecture is genuinely beautiful. Most of the restaurants and bars worth going to in Lincoln are either in the Haymarket or nearby. This is where you spend your time. If you're unsure where to go, start here and see what happens.

Free to explore · O & 7th St area · Walk everything · Day and night ↗ Maps
Lied Center for Performing Arts
🎭⭐ 4.9
Performing Arts · UNL Campus · World-Class Venue · Check Schedule

A world-class performing arts venue on the UNL campus that brings in artists and productions that a city of Lincoln's size has no business having access to — and yet here it is 🎭. Broadway tours, world-renowned orchestras, dance companies, comedy acts, and speaker series throughout the season. The acoustics are legitimately exceptional (it's one of the better rooms in the country). Check the season schedule before your visit — if there's something on while you're here, go.

🏆 Do: Check the season schedule before visiting · Buy tickets ahead · Dress comfortably
Ticket prices vary · UNL Campus · Check liedcenter.org for schedule ↗ Maps
Memorial Stadium — Husker Football
🏈⭐ 4.9
Big Ten Football · Game Day · UNL Campus · Sea of Red

On game days, Memorial Stadium becomes the third-largest city in Nebraska — 90,000+ people in a sea of red with an atmosphere that's legitimately one of the great environments in college football 🏈. Nebraska fans have sold out every home game since 1962 — the longest active sellout streak in college football history. If there's a home game while you're here, go. Even if you don't care about football. The tailgate culture, the stadium noise, and the spectacle of 90,000 people in one place is worth experiencing once. Tickets go fast.

🏆 Do: Tailgate in the morning · Wear red · Experience the stadium atmosphere
Ticket required · UNL Campus · Home game sellout streak since 1962 · Wear red ↗ Maps
National Museum of Roller Skating
🛼⭐ 4.5
Unique · South Lincoln · World's Only · Free

The world's only museum dedicated entirely to roller skating — and yes, it takes itself completely seriously, and yes, it's actually interesting 🛼. Artifacts spanning 200 years of the sport: vintage skates, skating costumes, championship memorabilia, early skating rink equipment. More fascinating than it sounds. Free admission. Takes about 45 minutes. Worth it for the "wait, this is real and in Lincoln?" energy alone. Tell people you went.

🏆 Do: Free admission · 45 min visit · Best conversation starter of the trip
Free · 4730 South St · Wed–Sat 11am–4pm · Mon–Tue/Sun closed ↗ Maps
🌽 UNL Campus tip: The Sheldon Museum of Art, International Quilt Museum, Lied Center, and Memorial Stadium are all on or adjacent to the University of Nebraska campus. A campus walk strings them all together in a half-day.

Art Galleries

Lincoln has a deeper gallery scene than most people expect. Working artist studios, co-op galleries, nationally ranked museums — and a monthly First Friday that turns the whole downtown into one big gallery walk.

By Zach

First Friday Art Walk

Every 1st Friday · 5–10pm

Every first Friday of the month, 50+ downtown galleries, studios, and businesses open their doors simultaneously — new exhibitions, artist receptions, free admission. The best single night on Lincoln's arts calendar. No tickets, no cover. Just show up and wander.

Haymarket & Downtown

Gallery District

The Haymarket and O Street corridor is the densest concentration of gallery space in Lincoln — walkable, eclectic, and best experienced on a First Friday when everything is open simultaneously.

The Burkholder Project
🏗️⭐ 4.8
Working Studios · 719 P St · Haymarket · 36 Studios · Since 1987

Lincoln's first Haymarket gallery — and still the most immersive. A converted 1880s carpet factory with 36 working artist studios across three levels of galleries, connected by a sun-filled atrium. You can watch painters paint, sculptors sculpt, photographers print 🎨. Four show openings every month. Two-time Best Gallery in Nebraska. This is what makes Lincoln's art scene different: artists actually here, actually working, actually willing to talk to you.

🏆 Do: Walk all three gallery levels · Watch artists at work · Ask questions — they want you to
Free · 719 P St · Haymarket · Wed–Fri 11am–5pm · Sat 10am–4pm · First Friday participant ↗ Maps
Kiechel Fine Art
🖼️⭐ 4.9
Fine Art · 1208 O St · Est. 1986 · American Masters + Contemporary

The blue-chip gallery of Lincoln's art scene — in business since 1986, representing estates of notable artists, placing work in museums and corporate collections worldwide. Specializes in 19th and 20th century American Art, Old Master prints, and regional contemporary 🖼️. Currently showing James Eisentrager (third floor) and the upcoming Chad M. Olsen "Color Fields" opening March 20 (5–8pm). Certified appraisal services on-site. Worth visiting just to see what serious gallery programming looks like.

🏆 Do: Ask about current exhibitions · Check upcoming opening receptions · Mar 20: Color Fields opening 5–8pm
Free · 1208 O St · Tue–Thu 10am–6pm · Fri 10am–8pm · Sat 11am–5pm · Sun/Mon closed ↗ Maps
Gallery 9
🎭⭐ 4.7
Co-op Gallery · 124 S 9th St · Mission Arts Building · Since 1994

An independent artist cooperative in the historic Mission Arts Building — one of the more interesting buildings in Lincoln's arts district, home to artist studios, arts-related businesses, and residencies 🎭. Gallery 9 has been running monthly solo, two-person, and group shows since 1994. The cooperative model means you're seeing work curated by and for working artists rather than commercial interests. Monthly schedule keeps the shows fresh.

🏆 Do: Check monthly show schedule · Solo shows give the work room to breathe
Free · 124 S 9th St · Wed–Sat 11am–5pm · Sun noon–5pm · Mon–Tue closed ↗ Maps
Clements Noyes Art Gallery
🎨⭐ 4.7
Co-op · 119 S 9th St · 100+ Artists · Since 1993 · Affordable

The most accessible gallery in Lincoln — 100+ local and regional artists in a warm co-op space near the Haymarket. Original paintings, photography, pottery, jewelry, and gifts at prices that don't require a long conversation with your accountant 🎨. Monthly workshops open to all skill levels. First Friday receptions are genuinely social: food, the artists present, an easy way into the local arts community. Great for buying art you'll actually live with.

🏆 Do: First Friday reception (6:30–9pm) · Browse the monthly show · Talk to the artists
Free · 119 S 9th St · Mon–Sat 10am–5pm · First Friday 6:30–9pm ↗ Maps
Metro Gallery Nebraska
🗺️⭐ 4.6
Large Collection · 1316 N St · Nebraska History Art · 5,000+ Works

Part gallery, part mini-museum of Lincoln and Nebraska. 5,000+ works of art on consignment plus original and reproduction maps of Nebraska dating to the 1850s that document the state's history in ways no photograph can 🗺️. The map collection alone is worth the visit for history enthusiasts. Cleaning, restoration, leasing, and custom exhibit services available. Monthly First Friday event. Underrated and often overlooked.

🏆 Do: Ask to see the historic map collection · First Friday monthly event
Free · 1316 N St #101 · Mon–Tue 11am–5:30pm · Wed 11am–7pm · Thu–Fri 11am–5:30pm · Sat noon–4pm ↗ Maps
Great Plains Art Museum
🌾⭐ 4.8
Regional Art · 1155 Q St · Prairie + Western · Free · UNL

Dedicated entirely to the art of the Great Plains — and it's more compelling than that description makes it sound. Bronzes, paintings, photography, and sculpture capturing the landscape, culture, and people of the American interior 🌾. Strong Native American and western art collections. Small but well-curated. Free admission. Often hosts First Friday programming. Worth 45 minutes for anyone with any interest in regionalism, landscape, or American western art.

🏆 Do: Bronze sculpture collection · Native American art galleries · Free and genuinely good
Free · 1155 Q St · Tue–Sat 10am–5pm · First Friday participant ↗ Maps
WallSpace LNK
📸⭐ 4.7
Contemporary · 1624 S 17th St · Nonprofit · Photography + Plains Art

A newer but fast-growing force in Lincoln's gallery scene — founded 2022, restructured as a nonprofit arts space in 2024. Monthly exhibitions of contemporary fine art with a focus on photography and plains-themed work 📸. Gallery talks, screenings, and artist receptions run alongside each show. The nonprofit model keeps it mission-driven rather than commercial. Open Fri–Sun only; check the calendar before visiting.

🏆 Do: Check the monthly show · Gallery talks are worth catching · Photography-forward exhibitions
Free · 1624 S 17th St Ste 300 · Fri–Sun noon–5pm · Monthly exhibitions ↗ Maps
Constellation Studios
🖨️⭐ 4.8
Printmaking · 2055 O St · Working Studio · Specialized · Since 2013

One of the most specialized fine art spaces in Lincoln — a working printmaking, papermaking, and bookmaking studio in a historic refurbished building 🖨️. Founded by internationally renowned printmaker Karen Kunc. The gallery showcases fine art prints; the classes and workshops teach traditional and innovative techniques to emerging artists. First Friday open studio hours are typically 6–8pm. A deep dive into a corner of the art world most people don't know exists.

🏆 Do: First Friday 6–8pm open studio · Ask about printmaking workshops · Unique specialty you won't find elsewhere
· 2055 O St · First Friday 6–8pm · Verify current hours · constellation-studios.net ↗ Maps
Michael Forsberg Gallery
🦅⭐ 4.8
Conservation Photography · 100 N 8th St · Great Plains Wildlife · National Geographic

The gallery of Nebraska conservation photographer Michael Forsberg — whose images have appeared in National Geographic, Audubon, and Nature Conservancy 🦅. Fine art prints of the Great Plains, Platte River crane migration, prairie ecosystems, and Nebraska wildlife. Work that has traveled nationally in solo exhibitions and ended up in public and private collections across the country. If you've ever felt the Great Plains landscape was worth paying attention to, this gallery will confirm it.

🏆 Do: Crane migration photography · Great Plains landscape series · Support a working conservation photographer
· 100 N 8th St · Mon–Sat 10am–6pm · First Friday participant ↗ Maps
Tugboat Gallery
⚓⭐ 4.7
Experimental · ~120 N 14th St · Non-Commercial · 500+ Artists Since 2005

The underground choice. Non-commercial, non-commission, all-volunteer gallery that has exhibited 500+ artists since 2005 ⚓. Exists specifically to give showing opportunities to experimental work, underrepresented artists, and nonconformist art that won't fit anywhere else. Located above Gomez Art Supply. A First Friday staple for people who want something different from the polished gallery circuit. Check tugboat.gallery for current hours — it runs on volunteer time.

🏆 Do: First Friday visit · Whatever's on rotation this month · Buy something weird
Free · ~120 N 14th St · Check tugboat.gallery for hours · First Friday staple ↗ Maps

Anchor Museums

World-Class Collection

Two museums that would be major draws in any city — and in Lincoln they're free, rarely crowded, and almost always have something genuinely worth seeing.

Sheldon Museum of Art
🎨⭐ 4.8
451 N 12th St · UNL · Philip Johnson Building · Free · World-Class

One of the best university art museums in the country, full stop. The Philip Johnson building (1963) is a landmark of modern architecture, and the collection inside earns it: 12,000+ works spanning 19th–20th century American art, with a sculpture garden featuring 30+ large-scale pieces 🎨. Current show: American Identity in Art (through July 5, 2026) — examining how artists reflect the American experience as the country marks its 250th anniversary. Free. Open six days a week. First Friday participant.

🏆 Do: American Identity in Art · Sculpture garden · Architecture as much as the collection inside
Free · 451 N 12th St · Tue–Wed/Fri–Sat 10am–5pm · Thu 10am–7pm · Sun noon–5pm ↗ Maps
LUX Center for the Arts
✨⭐ 4.7
Community Art Center · 2601 N 48th St · Free Gallery · 18+ Shows/Year

Lincoln's community art center — galleries, classes, and outreach all under one roof ✨. Free gallery exhibitions from local, regional, and national artists, with 18+ show rotations per year. The classes and workshops are open to all ages and all experience levels. Their Outreach Program brings art instruction to at-risk youth, low-income schools, and cancer patients. On First Fridays the gallery extends to noon–8pm with free opening receptions. More neighborhood center than museum — and that's what makes it work.

🏆 Do: First Friday extended hours noon–8pm · Check class schedule · Free gallery exhibitions
Free · 2601 N 48th St · Tue–Sat 10am–5pm · First Friday noon–8pm ↗ Maps
